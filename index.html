Way Of Working

Guided by DRY, KISS and SOLID, I aim for clean, maintainable code yet stay pragmatic: when deadlines outweigh architectural dividends, I deliver the simplest solution that works, flagging refactors for later. I write tests not to inflate coverage dashboards but to safeguard behaviour where failure hurts—payments, pricing, order flow. Easy-going with stakeholders, I never say “can’t”; instead I outline cost-benefit trade-offs, suggest lean alternatives, and provide clear impact estimates. The result is a steady cadence of value, low defect counts, and a team culture focused on outcomes, not dogma.
